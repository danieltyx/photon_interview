Something great starts here.

Welcome to the Photon interview process. We really liked your background and would love to learn more about how you think and work.

Three questions. For each you'll get 15 minutes to think, then 2 minutes to walk us through your idea out loud. You don't need to write a full solution — focus on explaining your approach. Code snippets are fine. Your camera, microphone, and screen are recorded the entire time. There are no retakes and no going back.

Use whatever tools help you — ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor, Google, Stack Overflow, your IDE — all of it. We want to see how you actually work, not how you perform without your tools. Just keep them on the screen we're recording.

One small piece of advice: don't paste the whole question into ChatGPT and read the output. Break it down. Ask a specific sub-question, then a follow-up. We'd much rather see you wrestle with the problem than recite a generated answer.

The first time might not be perfect — and that's the point. We value growth and learning more than a flawless first attempt. That's why round 2 exists: spend the time between rounds understanding and learning the things you'd never heard of, and come back stronger.
